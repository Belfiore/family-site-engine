The Moreaus come to London

A made-up family. Nothing here is a real person.

Lyon

Jean-Baptiste Moreau was born in Lyon in 1850 and wove silk there. He married Louise Fabre in 1877. The family says the silk trade in Lyon was failing, but no record says why they left.

The crossing

On 4 May 1878 Jean-Baptiste, aged 28, and Louise crossed from Calais to Dover on the Lord Warden. The passenger list gives his trade as silk weaver.

Greek Street

By the census of 1881 they lived at 9 Greek Street, Soho. Their son Henri was born there in 1879 and their daughter Claire in 1882. Henri became a tailor.

In September 1899 Henri sailed from Le Havre to Southampton on the Normandie, after a summer in France. In Southampton he met Edith Clarke, and they married in 1903.

The count

The family has always said the Moreaus descend from a Count Auguste de Moreau of Paris. No record links them, so he is not on the tree. A letter calls a Pierre Moreau of Lyon a cousin; he is shown on the tree beside Jean-Baptiste, with no proven link.
