Crosby family heritage

Tracy Jo (Magwire) Crosby and Richard "Rick" R. Crosby, and the families behind them: Magwire, Farr and West, Richardson, Pierce, and Spaulding and Putnam.

The Crosbys in this line come from Yarmouth County, Nova Scotia. The Magwires, Spauldings and Farrs are a Springfield, Vermont family. The Richardsons come through Chicopee and Wayland, Massachusetts. This site publishes what the records support, and it says when a link is only probable.

How to read the tree

A solid line is a link a record states: a birth register, a marriage register, a death registration, or the 1950 census naming a daughter in the household. A dashed line is probable. The records point to it, often an obituary or a compiled genealogy, and a certificate would make it firmer. The legend on the family tree says the same thing.

Five people are named in the research notes only as leads: George and Mary (Prentice) Magwire, Nathaniel Spalding, and Timothy Putnam and Betsey (Hall) Putnam. They are not drawn. The notes are on the research page.

Six people are living. The tree shows each name and how they are related, and nothing more: no dates, no places, no photographs, and no biography. They are not on the map. They are Tracy Jo (Magwire) Crosby, Richard "Rick" R. Crosby, their children Brittany, Mitch and Travis, and Alma (Farr) Cook.
